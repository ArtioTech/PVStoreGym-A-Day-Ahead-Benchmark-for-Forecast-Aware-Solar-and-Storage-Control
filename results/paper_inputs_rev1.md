# Paper inputs, protocol rev1 (final technical revision)

Every number below comes from a file in `results/rev1/` (generated by
`make benchmark` under `configs/journal.yaml`) or from a script named next to
it. Tables A–E, H, I and the RL details are generated by
`results/rev1/make_tables.py` (full output: `results/rev1/tables_rev1.md`) and
copied here unchanged. `main.tex` is not in the repository, so line numbers
refer to the marker numbering of `results/paper_inputs_v3.md`.

## What changed from v3 (CHANGELOG 2.1.0)

| Item | v3 | rev1 |
|---|---|---|
| Market time stamps (`market_clock`) | HEnEx/IPTO CET/CEST stamps read as Athens time: every market value one hour early | moved one hour later at load; IDA3 covers 13:00–01:00 local |
| Irradiance stamps (`radiation_stamp`) | hourly mean placed at the end of its hour | moved 30 min earlier, to the centre of the hour |
| Intraday reference of RBC and ID-MPC (`id_reference`) | PV forecast only | D-1 planned metered injection plus the PV forecast change |
| Commercial position (`id_position_limit`) | x and Y clipped separately to ±373 kW (x + Y up to 746 kW) | −373 ≤ x + Y ≤ 373 kW in the environment, the intraday MPC and both oracles |
| Delivery days | 363 (DST days excluded) | 362 (also 2025-01-01, whose first market hour is not in the data); train 168, validation 61, test 91, held-out 42 |
| ORACLE-DELIVER | test split, all tasks | test split, T1 and T2 (one shared oracle run) |
| RL | trained under v3 | retrained under rev1 (tuning on T1, 5 seeds × 5 tasks × 3 algorithms) |

## Key results (for abstract, §8 and §11)

- **A controller now beats DA-ONLY on the IQM.** DA-OPT-ID-MPC has the highest
  IQM of S on every task, with CIs above zero: T1 +0.0030 [+0.0011, +0.0049],
  T2 +0.0048 [+0.0020, +0.0075], T3 +0.0032 [+0.0012, +0.0051], T4 +0.0031
  [+0.0011, +0.0050], T5 +0.0051 [+0.0022, +0.0079]. DA-OPT-RBC: CI above zero
  on T2 and T5, includes zero on T1, T3, T4. DA-OPT-MPC: below zero on T1, T3,
  T4, CI includes zero on T2 and T5. DA-OPT-RT-MPC below zero everywhere.
- **The mean profit says otherwise.** ID-MPC earns less than DA-ONLY on every
  task (T1 370.0 vs 373.7 EUR/day, CIs overlap); RBC earns 0.8–1.3 EUR/day more
  (CIs overlap). ID-MPC has a heavier lower tail (CVaR₅% 137.9 vs 178.8 EUR on
  T1). The scores are tiny either way: 0.3–0.5% of the headroom U − J_L.
- **S_D on T1/T2:** ID-MPC +0.0124 [+0.0028, +0.0206] and +0.0174 [+0.0043,
  +0.0297]; RBC +0.0021 [−0.0009, +0.0049] and +0.0061 [+0.0013, +0.0109].
- **RL is below DA-ONLY on every task** (IQM of S −0.0972 to −0.1470) and below
  its v3 scores on 14 of 15 task × algorithm pairs (Table A2). T2 SAC has a mean
  profit of −197.5 EUR/day [−618.7, 122.2] because of penalty episodes
  (456.04 EUR/day of penalties on average).
- **Bounds:** U falls from 1741.8 (v3) to 1231.7 EUR/day on T1, mainly
  because of the position bound in the oracle. 1042.9 EUR/day of the ORACLE
  incumbent is imbalance revenue (Table B2). U_D = 557.5 EUR/day (v3: 575.1).
- **Over-selling after A1:** the intraday rule no longer over-sells (scheduled
  1705.2 vs metered 1793.5 kWh/day, like DA-ONLY 1702.2 / 1791.0). The intraday
  MPC still does: scheduled 2288.2 vs metered 1818.9 (ID-MPC) and 1762.5 (MPC)
  kWh/day, imbalance −107.8 and −108.1 EUR/day. Its intraday revenue (111.2
  EUR/day) is almost exactly offset by imbalance cost.
- **Layer attribution, T1, S:** Δ_ID +0.0078 [−0.0038, +0.0200] (CI includes
  zero), Δ_RT −0.0079 [−0.0118, −0.0040], interaction +0.0105 [+0.0070, +0.0144].
